# soapff library: ClayFF (Cygan, Liang, Kalinichev 2004) for minerals, water and ions and OPLS-AA (Jorgensen, Maxwell,
# Tirado-Rives 1996, as distributed with Foyer) for organic molecules, as one force field, as Szczerba & Kalinichev (Clays
# Clay Miner. 64, 488 (2016)) put the two together: the LJ of unlike atoms by the Lorentz-Berthelot rules (arithmetic sigma,
# geometric epsilon) for every pair, the 1-4 pairs of OPLS-AA scaled by 0.5 (ClayFF has none).
#
# Types and charges in two steps: the bonds from the distance and the bond length of each pair of elements ([bond_lengths]),
# and the type and charge from the SOAP of the atoms within 4 bonds; the charges are those of the reference atoms (ClayFF
# Table 1; OPLS-AA gives every type one charge).  The metals and bridging O of ClayFF are named in no row of [bonds]: their
# bonds of step 1 only give the SOAP its neighbourhood, and the force field has no metal-O bond.
#
# References (clayff_oplsaa/): polymers/, oligomers of 11 polymers and their MD frames at 413 K typed by Foyer; bulk/ and
# basal/, 11 crystals of 9 layered minerals and their (001) slabs; water/, water and the ions of ClayFF Table 1 in bulk
# water and in films and a droplet in vacuum.
# Parameters: ClayFF Tables 1 and 2 (the flexible SPC water); OPLS-AA of foyer 1.3.0 forcefields/xml/oplsaa.xml.
