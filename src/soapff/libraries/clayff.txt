# soapff library: ClayFF (Cygan, Liang, Kalinichev, J. Phys. Chem. B 108, 1255 (2004)), the default clay library of soapff.
#
# engine clayff: soapff_cui, in the two steps of the molecular engine (soapff_cui/steps.py).  1. The bonds from the distance
# and the bond length of each pair of elements ([bond_lengths] below: halfway between the longest bond and the shortest
# distance of two atoms not bonded in the references, H-O by hand); the ions are bonded to nothing
# (no ion is bonded in the references).  2. The SOAP of the atoms within 4 bonds only (water, ions and the next layer left
# out of a mineral atom's SOAP): the nearest reference atom gives the ClayFF type, and ClayFF Table 1 its charge.  The
# water molecules (an O bonded to two H only: in the references only the O of water has two H) are taken apart from the
# mineral in step 1, so that the mineral is typed without them and each molecule on its own bonds.  LJ, the O-H bond and the H-O-H / M-O-H angles of
# Tables 1 and 2 (soapff_cui/clayff.py), with the flexible SPC water; the O-H bonds and M-O-H angles of the force field
# come from the bonds of step 1.
#
# References: the 11 bulk layered minerals, their basal (0 0 1) slabs in vacuum, water and the ions (LAMMPS data files with the
# ClayFF type names in the comments of Masses).  Their bonds: the O-H bonds of the files and, as ClayFF has no metal-O bond,
# each Si, Al, Mg bonded to the O within 2.5 A (4 or 6 O within 2.19 A, the next O beyond 3.14 A).  The slab rows are pruned
# at similarity 0.999, the water rows at 0.99.  To add a mineral, make your own library:
#     soapff.new_library("myclay", extends="clayff");  soapff.add_reference("myclay", "mymineral.data")
# (the data file of the mineral with its ClayFF types and charges, like the files in clayff/bulk/).

[library]
name        = clayff
engine      = clayff
description = ClayFF (Cygan et al. 2004): 11 layered minerals (montmorillonite, micas, kaolinite, talc, brucite, ...), their basal surfaces, water and the ions of ClayFF Table 1
reference   = R. T. Cygan, J.-J. Liang, A. G. Kalinichev, J. Phys. Chem. B 108, 1255 (2004)
prebuilt    = clayff/prebuilt/library_clayff_bulk.npz
prebuilt_sha = 670ea9888a93c5f4
prebuilt_dscribe = 2.1.2

[soap]
r_cut = 5.0
n_max = 4
l_max = 3
sigma = 0.4
depth = 4
threshold = 0.9

[bond_lengths]
# E1 E2 r(A): two atoms of these elements nearer than r are bonded (step 1).  r lies between the longest bond (d_b)
# and the shortest distance of two atoms not bonded (d_n) in the references (checked when the table is built); the
# metal-O pairs halfway.  H-O by hand: in MD at 300 K the H...O of the hydrogen bonds comes to 1.43 A (in the references,
# packed water, not nearer than 2.04 A) and the O-H bonds of the flexible SPC water stretch to 1.12 A.
# E1 E2  r       d_b    d_n
O  Si  2.41   # 1.683  3.143
Al O   2.66   # 2.105  3.213
Mg O   2.70   # 2.187  3.217
H  O   1.25   # 1.050  2.039  (halfway 1.54)
